Project overview

Android App / Mapping & Wildlife Reporting

Built an Android application concept for trekkers and forest department users to log animal sightings, support field reporting, and improve wildlife location awareness.

Android • Kotlin/Java • Maps • Mobile UI • Location Tracking

Highlights

  • Included map-based reporting concept for wildlife location awareness.
  • Supported structured field data entry for animal sightings.
  • Designed the app for outdoor, field, and conservation-focused usage.
  • Created the foundation for future location-based alerts and forest department monitoring workflows.

This project aims to improve wildlife reporting and safety by giving trekkers and forest teams a structured way to log sightings.
