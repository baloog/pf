Mental Health ML / Multi-task Learning

Extended Project4Lonely into a multi-task learning pipeline predicting loneliness, depression, stress, and social connectedness using behavioral and physiological signals.

Python • XGBoost • MultiOutput Models • SHAP • Pandas • Scikit-learn

Highlights

  • Added multi-task prediction for loneliness, depression, stress, and social connectedness.
  • Used MultiOutput XGBoost and single-target comparison workflows.
  • Evaluated median split label strategy.
  • Added SHAP explainability and correlation analysis.
  • Found that physiological readiness and sleep features mattered more than phone usage signals.
  • Compared multi-task and single-target modeling performance.
